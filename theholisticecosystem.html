About Me

My name is Jamie, and my journey has been anything but conventional.

Over the years, I have experienced more than 70 psychiatric hospitalizations, survived more than 15 overdoses, worked over 30 different jobs, experienced homelessness multiple times, rebuilt my life repeatedly, and navigated the challenges of ADHD, OCD, CPTSD, Autism, PMDD, and Major Depressive Disorder.

I am not a licensed therapist, psychologist, or physician.

What I do bring is more than 15 years of personal growth, therapy, research, education, and lived experience. I have spent countless hours learning about mental health, physical wellness, behavior change, nutrition, resilience, and personal development not because it was a hobby, but because my life depended on it.

Today, I am pursuing higher education, building wellness-focused resources through The Holistic Ecosystem, writing books, developing a podcast, and creating content designed to help others improve both their mental and physical well-being.

I know what it feels like to be overwhelmed, isolated, misunderstood, and exhausted from trying to hold everything together. I know what it is like to start over, to rebuild after setbacks, and to wonder whether things will ever get better. I also know that healing is rarely a straight line. Progress often looks messy, imperfect, and frustratingly slow. Yet it is still possible.

Everything I share on this website comes from a combination of lived experience, ongoing education, evidence-based information, and a genuine desire to help others find practical ways to move forward. My goal is not perfection. My goal is progress, growth, and helping people discover what works for them.

I cannot replace a therapist, nor do I claim to have all the answers.

What I can do is offer encouragement, share what I have learned, provide practical tools and resources, and help you feel less alone in whatever challenges you may be facing.

Above all else, that is the mission of The Holistic Ecosystem.

For many years I have been working on living a holistic lifestyle, which has been a lot of ups and downs. I would be lying if I said it was an easy change. It has been anything but easy for me to really make changes which make my life better.

One thing I have learned along the way is that no matter what anyone says, no one specific diet, financial plan, budget, coping skill, or exercise plan works the same for everyone. What works for me will not work for everyone else. The benefit to having a life coach like myself is that I will work with you to find what is best for you. As someone who is also neurodivergent, I can help you if you also fall into that category.

Whether you are neurodivergent or neurotypical, I want to help you find your personal path to a successful future of holistic health.
