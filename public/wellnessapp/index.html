Why Financial Wellness?

Money should support your life—not control it.

Financial health is about reducing stress, increasing freedom, and living intentionally. It goes beyond simply budgeting: it helps you understand your choices, prepare for uncertainty, and direct your money toward the life you want to build.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Financial Wellness?

It is a connected set of tools and educational resources designed to help you understand your finances, reduce stress, and make intentional progress.

Can I start for free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you the core tools needed to begin organizing your financial life and building healthier money habits.

Is this only a budgeting app?

No. Budgeting is one part of financial wellness. The experience also supports goals, debt reduction, emergency savings, net worth, and financial education.

What will Premium include?

Premium is planned to include more advanced insights, expanded planning tools, and personalized guidance.

Does this replace professional financial advice?

No. Financial Wellness provides educational tools and general guidance; it is not a substitute for individualized advice from a qualified professional.
