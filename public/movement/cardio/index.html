Cardio Resources

Cardio is any rhythmic movement that gets your heart and breathing working a little more. It can be gentle, seated, playful, low-impact, moderate, or intense depending on your body and goals.

Why This Matters

Cardio can support endurance, heart health, mood, sleep, stress relief, and daily energy. It does not have to be extreme to count.

Practical Ideas

Low Impact

Try walking, water walking, cycling, swimming, or gentle dancing.

Playful Cardio

Use music, games, sports, hiking, or active chores.

Intervals

Alternate short movement periods with rest breaks.

Adaptive Options

Try seated cardio, arm movements, or water-based activity.

Safety & Pacing Notes

Use the talk test as a gentle guide: moderate activity usually lets you talk but not sing. Slow down or stop if symptoms feel unsafe.

Try This This Week

  • Try one 10-minute cardio session this week.
  • Choose something enjoyable, not something you dread.
  • Add rest breaks as needed.
