Functional Movement

What Is Functional Movement?

Functional movement focuses on movement patterns used in everyday life, such as standing, reaching, lifting, carrying, balancing, and changing positions.

Why It Matters

Functional movement can support independence, daily comfort, confidence, posture, balance, coordination, and the ability to do ordinary tasks with less strain.

Beginner Goal

Practice movements that support daily life. Start with the activities you already do and build awareness slowly.

Examples

Squats

Reaching

Lifting

Carrying

Balance

Posture

Core Strength

Tips for Getting Started

Use Daily Tasks

Standing from a chair, carrying groceries, or reaching shelves can become gentle practice.

Move With Control

Slow, steady movement can help build confidence and awareness.

Support Balance

Use a wall, counter, chair, or another stable surface when needed.

Movement Options

Easy Options

Supported sit-to-stands, gentle reaching, posture practice, simple balance exercises, or light household tasks.

Moderate Options

Squats, carrying, Tai Chi, dance, core work, and coordinated movement patterns for daily life.

Intense Options

Agility drills, athletic coordination training, complex carries, or advanced balance challenges.

Chair & Wheelchair Options

Seated coordination, reaction exercises, reaching, adaptive carrying, posture work, and supported core movement.
