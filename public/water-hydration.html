Water & Hydration

What Is Hydration?

Hydration refers to maintaining adequate fluid levels within the body. Water is essential for nearly every bodily function, including temperature regulation, circulation, digestion, nutrient transport, and overall health.

Daily Hydration Recommendations

Women

Approximately 2.7 liters per day

Men

Approximately 3.7 liters per day

Fluid needs vary based on exercise, climate, illness, medications, pregnancy, and other factors.

Signs of Dehydration

Thirst

Dark Urine

Headache

Fatigue

Dizziness

Dry Mouth

Electrolytes Overview

Electrolytes are minerals that help regulate fluid balance, nerve function, muscle function, and heart function.

Important electrolytes include:

Sodium

Potassium

Magnesium

Calcium

Chloride

Phosphorus
