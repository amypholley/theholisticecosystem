Physical Wellness

Sleep & Recovery

Sleep and recovery are essential parts of physical wellness. Rest allows the body and mind to repair, reset, and prepare for daily life. Healthy recovery includes quality sleep, stress management, rest days, and routines that support long-term well-being.

Sleep & Recovery Essentials

Daily Sleep Recommendations:

  • Most adults need 7-9 hours of sleep per night.
  • Teenagers generally need 8-10 hours.
  • Children generally need 9-12 hours.

Recovery Basics:

  • Take rest seriously.
  • Build a consistent sleep routine.
  • Allow time for physical and mental recovery.
  • Reduce overstimulation before bed when possible.

Beginner Goal:

  • Start with a consistent bedtime and wake-up time.
