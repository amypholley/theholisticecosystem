Protein

Protein supports muscle repair, immune function, hormones, energy, fullness, and overall health. This page gives a quick overview of daily protein needs and common high-protein foods.

Daily Protein Needs

Most adults need about 0.8 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day as a general minimum. Some people may need more based on activity level, age, health goals, pregnancy, recovery, or medical needs.

Protein needs vary. This information is educational only and is not medical advice.
