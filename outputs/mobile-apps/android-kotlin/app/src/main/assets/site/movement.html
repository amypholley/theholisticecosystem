Movement

Movement is an essential part of physical wellness. Regular movement supports physical health, mental well-being, energy levels, mobility, and overall quality of life. Whether you are just getting started or looking to stay active, every movement counts.

Daily Movement Essentials

Weekly Recommendations:

  • Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate physical activity each week.
  • Include strength training at least 2 days per week.
  • Incorporate flexibility and mobility exercises regularly.
  • Reduce long periods of sitting when possible.

Beginner Goal:

  • Start with a 10-15 minute walk each day.
