Coping Skills

Coping skills are practical tools that can help people manage stress, regulate emotions, ground themselves, and get through difficult moments. Different skills work for different people, so this page offers many options to explore.

If you are in immediate danger or feel like you may harm yourself, call emergency services or contact a crisis hotline such as 988 in the United States.

100 Coping Skills

Grounding Skills

  1. Use the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding method
  2. Name five things you can see
  3. Hold an ice cube
  4. Count backward from 100
  5. Focus on your feet touching the floor
  6. Describe the room around you
  7. Touch something textured
  8. Listen for five different sounds
  9. Name objects by color
  10. Repeat a calming phrase

Breathing Skills

  1. Box breathing
  2. Deep belly breathing
  3. 4-7-8 breathing
  4. Slow exhales
  5. Count each breath
  6. Breathe in through the nose and out through the mouth
  7. Place a hand on your chest and stomach
  8. Practice paced breathing
  9. Imagine breathing in calm and out stress
  10. Pair breathing with calming music

Physical Coping Skills

  1. Go for a walk
  2. Stretch
  3. Take a shower
  4. Drink water
  5. Eat a nourishing snack
  6. Dance to one song
  7. Do gentle yoga
  8. Clean one small area
  9. Sit outside
  10. Play with a pet

Creative Coping Skills

  1. Journal
  2. Draw
  3. Color
  4. Paint
  5. Write poetry
  6. Make a playlist
  7. Take photos
  8. Create a collage
  9. Work on a craft
  10. Write a letter you do not send

Emotional Regulation Skills

  1. Name the emotion
  2. Rate the emotion from 1 to 10
  3. Ask what the emotion is trying to tell you
  4. Practice self-compassion
  5. Use opposite action
  6. Take a pause before responding
  7. Identify the trigger
  8. Write down the thought
  9. Challenge an unhelpful thought
  10. Remind yourself feelings are temporary

  1. Do a puzzle
  2. Read a book
  3. Listen to a podcast
  4. Watch a comforting show
  5. Learn something new
  6. Make a simple to-do list
  7. Break a task into smaller steps
  8. Practice mindfulness
  9. Use a grounding app
  10. Repeat affirmations

Social Coping Skills

  1. Text a trusted person
  2. Call a friend

  4. Spend time with safe people

  6. Sit near others in a public place
  7. Talk to a therapist
  8. Write in an online support community
  9. Ask someone to distract you

  1. Wrap in a blanket

  3. Light a candle safely

  5. Hold a stuffed animal or pillow

  7. Listen to calming sounds
  8. Use lotion or calming scents
  9. Put on comfortable clothes
  10. Rest without guilt

  2. Do one small chore
  3. Make the bed

  5. Organize one drawer
  6. Pay one bill

  8. Create a simple meal

  3. Pray
  4. Meditate
  5. Read something inspirational

  7. Write a gratitude list

  9. Remember a hard thing you survived
