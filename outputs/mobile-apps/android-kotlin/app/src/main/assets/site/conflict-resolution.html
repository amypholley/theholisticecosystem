Conflict Resolution

Conflict is a normal part of relationships because people have different needs, histories, perspectives, and limits. Healthy conflict resolution focuses on safety, understanding, accountability, problem solving, and repair rather than winning.

Understanding Conflict

Why Conflict Happens

Differences in needs, expectations, communication, stress, roles, values, resources, or interpretation can create conflict.

Healthy Conflict

People can disagree while remaining respectful, listening, maintaining safety, and allowing each person a voice.

Unhealthy Conflict

Threats, coercion, contempt, intimidation, violence, manipulation, repeated humiliation, and retaliation are not healthy conflict.

Conflict Patterns

Notice pursuit and withdrawal, escalation, avoidance, scorekeeping, blame, defensiveness, or unresolved repetition.

Communication During Conflict

De-Escalation

Lower volume, slow speech, reduce the number of issues, and pause when regulation or safety is deteriorating.

Taking Breaks

Agree on how long the break will last and when the conversation will resume.

Active Listening

Reflect what you heard before responding and ask what the other person most wants understood.

Finding Common Ground

Identify shared concerns, values, or outcomes even when preferred solutions differ.

Problem Solving Together

Define one problem, brainstorm options, choose a trial, and review whether it helped.

Repairing After Conflict

Apologies

Name the action and impact, express regret, avoid excuses, and describe what you will do differently.

Accountability

Accept consequences, follow through, seek feedback, and change repeated behavior.

Compromise

Look for fair tradeoffs without pressuring anyone to surrender safety, consent, or core boundaries.

Rebuilding Trust

Trust returns through repeated reliable behavior, transparency where appropriate, and time.

When to Seek Help

Use qualified support when conflict is chronic, overwhelming, or difficult to resolve; prioritize specialist safety help for abuse.
