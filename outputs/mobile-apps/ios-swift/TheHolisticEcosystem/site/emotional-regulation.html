Emotional Regulation

Emotional regulation includes tools for calming the nervous system, managing intense emotions, and responding instead of reacting.

Regulation does not mean ignoring emotions or staying calm all the time. It means noticing what is happening, creating enough space to choose a response, and using support when emotions feel difficult to manage alone.

Foundations of Emotional Regulation

Notice the Emotion

Name the feeling, notice where it shows up in your body, and identify what may have triggered it. Awareness can make the next step clearer.

Pause and Settle

Create a brief pause with slower breathing, grounding, movement, quiet, or sensory support. The goal is to reduce intensity, not force the feeling away.

Choose a Response

Consider what the situation needs, what is within your control, and which action matches your values. A response may include communication, a boundary, rest, or asking for help.
