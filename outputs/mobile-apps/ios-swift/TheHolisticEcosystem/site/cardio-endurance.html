Cardio & Endurance

What Are Cardio & Endurance?

Cardio and endurance activities are movements that gently challenge the heart, lungs, and stamina over time. They can be low-impact, moderate, playful, or broken into short sessions.

Why They Matter

Cardio and endurance can support heart health, circulation, energy, stamina, mood, and confidence with daily activities.

Beginner Goal

Work toward 150 minutes of moderate activity per week over time. It is okay to begin with a few minutes and build slowly.

Examples

Brisk Walking

Cycling

Swimming

Dancing

Hiking

Jogging

Tips for Getting Started

Break It Up

Short sessions can count and may feel more realistic than one long workout.

Choose Low Impact

Walking, cycling, swimming, and dancing can be easier on the body.

Follow Your Energy

Adjust pace and duration based on fatigue, stress, pain, and recovery needs.
