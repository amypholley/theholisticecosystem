Strength & Resistance Training

What Is Strength & Resistance Training?

Strength and resistance training uses muscles against resistance. That resistance can come from bodyweight, bands, weights, machines, or everyday objects.

Why Strength Matters

Strength supports everyday function, joint stability, posture, balance, and independence. It can help daily tasks feel more manageable, especially when built slowly and consistently.

Beginner Goal

Begin with 2 days per week. Start light, practice form, and allow recovery between sessions.

Tips for Getting Started

Choose Simple Moves

Start with basic movements like sitting and standing, wall pushups, rows, or gentle carries.

Use Gentle Resistance

Light effort is enough at first. Progress can happen slowly.

Rest Matters

Give your body time to recover, especially with fatigue, pain, or chronic illness.

Movement Options

Easy Options

Wall push-ups, sit-to-stands, light resistance bands, gentle carries, or supported bodyweight movements.

Moderate Options

Bodyweight exercises, kettlebells, dumbbells, resistance machines, or gradually heavier bands.

Intense Options

Powerlifting, advanced strength programs, heavier resistance, or performance-focused training.

Chair & Wheelchair Options

Seated resistance-band exercises, adaptive strength training, seated presses, rows, and supported upper-body work.
