Flexibility & Mobility

What Are Flexibility & Mobility?

Flexibility is the ability of muscles to lengthen, while mobility is the ability to move joints through a comfortable range of motion. Both can be gentle and adaptable.

Why They Matter

Flexibility and mobility can support comfort, posture, daily movement, joint health, and body awareness. They may also help reduce stiffness from sitting, stress, or low activity days.

Beginner Goal

Try 5-10 minutes a few times per week. Gentle movement is enough to begin.

Examples

Stretching

Yoga

Mobility Drills

Gentle Range-of-Motion Exercises

Tips for Getting Started

Stay Comfortable

A stretch should feel gentle, not forced or painful.

Use Your Breath

Slow breathing can make movement feel calmer and more grounded.

Adapt Freely

Use chairs, walls, pillows, or shorter sessions when needed.
