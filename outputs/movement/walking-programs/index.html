Walking Programs

Walking can be simple, flexible, and surprisingly powerful. It can happen outside, in a hallway, at a store, on a treadmill, with a dog, with a friend, or in tiny pieces throughout the day.

Why This Matters

Walking can support heart health, energy, mood, stress relief, mobility, and daily stamina. It is also easy to adjust by changing pace, distance, location, support, or rest breaks.

Practical Ideas

Easy Start

Walk for 5-10 minutes at a comfortable pace.

Everyday Routes

Try mailbox walks, hallway laps, mall walking, or dog walks.

Make It Pleasant

Use music, podcasts, nature, prayer, or a walking buddy.

Gentle Progress

Add 1-2 minutes when your body feels ready.

Safety & Pacing Notes

Choose safe surfaces, supportive shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, and routes that fit your comfort. Use mobility aids, benches, or rest stops when helpful.

Try This This Week

  • Take three short walks this week.
  • Keep each walk easy enough that you could repeat it.
  • Celebrate showing up, even if the walk is short.
