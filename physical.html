Physical Health

"Physical fitness is not only one of the most important keys to a healthy body, it is the basic of dynamic and creative intellectual activity." -John F. Kennedy

Physical health is the care and attention we give our bodies through movement, rest, nourishment, hydration, medical care, and routines that can be repeated in real life.

Nutrition

Gentle, realistic nourishment that supports energy, routines, and daily life.

Movement

Exercise and body movement that can be adapted to your needs and capacity.

Sleep and Recovery

Rest, hydration, recovery, and sustainable habits that help the body reset.

Body Health

Body health focuses on specific areas and systems of the body, including the heart, lungs, skin, eyes, gut, thyroid, joints, bones, and more. These pages are designed to provide beginner-friendly education, practical wellness tips, and trusted resources from qualified health professionals and reputable medical organizations.

This information is educational only and is not medical advice. Always talk with a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, or medical concerns.

These pages will include a mix of original Holistic Ecosystem content and carefully selected resources from trusted health professionals, medical organizations, researchers, and published experts. The goal is to help visitors learn without feeling overwhelmed.

Click any section below for more information.

Major Body Systems

Nervous System

The nervous system includes the brain, spinal cord, and nerves.

Cardiovascular System

The cardiovascular system includes the heart, blood, and blood vessels.

Respiratory System

The respiratory system includes the airways, lungs, breathing muscles, and connected blood vessels.

Digestive System

The digestive system includes the gastrointestinal tract, liver, pancreas, and gallbladder.

Endocrine System

The endocrine system is a network of glands that release hormones.

Immune System

The immune system is a network of cells, tissues, organs, and signaling molecules that helps protect the body from infections and respond to injury..

Musculoskeletal System

The musculoskeletal system includes bones, muscles, joints, tendons, ligaments, and related tissues.

Reproductive System

The reproductive system includes organs and hormones involved in sexual development, fertility, menstruation, pregnancy, and reproductive health.

Urinary System

The urinary system includes the kidneys, ureters, bladder, and urethra.

Integumentary System

The integumentary system includes the skin, hair, nails, sweat glands, and related structures.

Lymphatic System

The lymphatic system includes lymph vessels, lymph nodes, the spleen, thymus, tonsils, and lymph fluid.

Sensory Systems

The sensory systems include vision, hearing, smell, taste, touch, balance, and internal body awareness.
