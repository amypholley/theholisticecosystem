Walking

What Is Walking?

Walking is a gentle, accessible form of movement that can be adapted to many energy levels, environments, and abilities. It can happen outdoors, indoors, on a treadmill, around a home, or in short movement breaks throughout the day.

Benefits of Walking

Walking can support heart health, circulation, mobility, mood, focus, stress relief, and daily energy. It can also be a helpful way to add movement without needing special equipment or intense exercise.

Beginner Goal

Start with 10-15 minutes per day, or less if that feels more realistic. A few minutes still counts.

Tips for Getting Started

Start Small

Begin with a distance or time that feels doable today.

Use Supports

Comfortable shoes, rest breaks, mobility aids, or a safe route can make walking easier.

Make It Gentle

Walking does not need to be fast or intense to support wellness.
