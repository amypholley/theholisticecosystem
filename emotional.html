Emotional Wellness

"Surround yourself with what you love, whether it's family, pets, keepsakes, music, plants, hobbies, whatever." -George Carlin

Emotional wellness is the ability to notice, understand, express, and care for your emotions in healthy ways. It includes building awareness, learning regulation skills, creating space for joy, and finding hobbies that support healing and balance.
