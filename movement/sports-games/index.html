Sports & Games Resources

Sports and games can turn movement into play. Bowling, batting cages, mini golf, pickleball, basketball, volleyball, tennis, golf, laser tag, swimming, Frisbee, and disc golf can all count as movement.

Why This Matters

Enjoyable movement is often easier to continue. Games and recreational sports can support coordination, endurance, confidence, connection, stress relief, and plain old fun.

Practical Ideas

Keep It Casual

Choose friendly games over pressure, performance, or perfection.

Modify the Rules

Shorten the game, lower intensity, change equipment, or take extra turns resting.

Invite Connection

Play with family, friends, coworkers, or community groups.

Choose Your Energy Level

Mini golf, bowling, swimming, disc golf, and pickleball can all be adjusted.

Safety & Pacing Notes

Warm up, hydrate, take breaks, and adapt for pain, fatigue, heat, balance, or mobility needs. Friendly competition should still leave room for kindness and rest.

Try This This Week

  • Pick one sport or game that sounds fun.
  • Plan a low-pressure way to try it this month.
  • Invite someone who will make it feel supportive.
