Strength Training Resources

Strength training includes bodyweight exercises, resistance bands, dumbbells, machines, household items, and adaptive strength options.

Why This Matters

Building strength can support daily tasks, joint stability, metabolism, posture, independence, and confidence. It can be gentle, moderate, or advanced depending on your needs.

Practical Beginner-Friendly Ideas

  • Try wall push-ups, sit-to-stands, or resistance band rows.
  • Begin with 1-2 sets of a few simple exercises.
  • Practice movements that support daily life, like lifting and carrying.
  • Rest between sessions so your body can recover.

Safety & Pacing Notes

Use slow controlled movement, start light, and avoid holding your breath. If you are unsure about form or safety, consider guidance from a qualified professional.
