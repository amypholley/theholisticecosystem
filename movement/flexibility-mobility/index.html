Flexibility & Mobility Resources

Flexibility and mobility work can help the body move more comfortably. These practices may include stretching, gentle range-of-motion exercises, yoga, mobility drills, or seated movement.

Why This Matters

Maintaining comfortable movement can support posture, joint health, daily function, relaxation, and injury prevention.

Practical Beginner-Friendly Ideas

  • Try 5 minutes of gentle stretching a few times per week.
  • Move slowly through comfortable ranges of motion.
  • Use seated stretches if standing is difficult.
  • Pair stretching with breathing, prayer, quiet music, or a calming routine.

Safety & Pacing Notes

Stretching should feel gentle, not sharp or forced. Avoid bouncing, and adapt positions to your body and mobility needs.
