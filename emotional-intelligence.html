Emotional Intelligence

What Is Emotional Intelligence?

Emotional intelligence is the ability to recognize emotions, understand what they may be communicating, and respond in ways that support well-being and relationships. It includes awareness of your own emotional patterns as well as sensitivity to the feelings, needs, and perspectives of others. These skills can be practiced over time; they are not a measure of whether someone is naturally calm, social, or emotionally expressive.

Self-Awareness

Notice what you feel and how emotions influence thoughts, behavior, and physical sensations.

  • Name emotions with greater precision
  • Recognize personal triggers
  • Notice recurring emotional patterns
  • Understand needs and strengths

Self-Regulation

Create enough space to respond thoughtfully without denying, suppressing, or acting on every feeling.

  • Pause before reacting
  • Use grounding and calming tools
  • Recover after emotional stress
  • Choose responses that fit your values

Social Awareness

Understand other perspectives while maintaining boundaries and avoiding assumptions about what someone feels.

  • Listen with curiosity
  • Notice verbal and nonverbal cues
  • Practice empathy and validation
  • Clarify instead of assuming

Practice in Everyday Life

Try a brief emotional check-in: What am I feeling? What happened before this feeling? What do I need, and what response would be respectful to both me and the people involved?
